Launching a website is the start, not the finish. Here's what keeping one running actually costs, so there are no surprises.

## The essentials

- **Domain name:** around $20 to $40 a year for a .com.au
- **Hosting or platform fees:** roughly $10 to $60 a month for most small business sites
- **Email:** often a separate Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 subscription
- **SSL certificate:** free on most modern platforms

## Maintenance tasks

**Platform sites (Squarespace, Shopify, Wix, Webflow)** handle updates and security for you, so maintenance is mostly content changes and occasional design refreshes.

**WordPress sites** need regular plugin, theme and core updates, backups and security monitoring. Skip these and you risk hacks and breakages.

## DIY vs a care plan

You can do it yourself if you're comfortable logging in monthly to update and check things. Many businesses prefer a care plan where someone else handles updates, backups, uptime monitoring and a bank of time for small changes.

## Hidden costs to watch

- Premium plugins or apps with annual licences
- Transaction fees on online stores
- Cheap hosting that slows your site and hurts SEO
- Emergency fixes when nobody's been maintaining the site

## A realistic budget

For most small businesses, plan for a few hundred dollars a year in running costs, plus a care plan or your own time for upkeep. It's a small price compared with a site that's slow, insecure or out of date.
