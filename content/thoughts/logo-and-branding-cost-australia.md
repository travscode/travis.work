I've branded wineries, bars, architecture studios and startups. Here's how logo and branding pricing breaks down in Australia, and what you actually get for your money.

## The ranges

- **DIY tools and AI generators:** cheap or free, but generic and hard to trademark
- **Design marketplaces:** low cost, very mixed quality, little strategy
- **Experienced freelance designer:** commonly from around $1,000 to $3,000 for a logo, more for a full identity
- **Agency:** significantly more, often with research and strategy teams

My brand identity projects start from $1,200, with a fixed quote and monthly payments available.

## What you should get from a professional

- Some discovery: who you are, who you're for, what makes you different
- Several directions explored, then refined into one
- Final files in every format you need (print, web, social)
- Colour and typography choices that work together
- Simple guidelines so it stays consistent

## When a full identity is worth it

If you're launching a product on a shelf, opening a venue or competing in a crowded market, the brand does a lot of selling for you. Wine labels are a great example: I named and branded [Fervor](/work/fervor) and redesigned [Skigh Wine's Coda range](/work/coda), where the label is often the reason someone picks the bottle up.

## How to keep costs down

1. Be clear on what you need now versus later
2. Start with the core identity, add extras as you grow
3. Prepare a short brief: your story, audience, competitors and likes or dislikes
