I get asked this question more than any other, usually a little nervously, as if the answer might be "how much have you got?" So here's a straight answer, from someone who builds websites in Perth for a living.

## The short answer

For a small business website built by a professional in Perth WA, you're typically looking at:

- **DIY builder (Squarespace, Wix):** your time, plus around $20 to $60 a month
- **Freelancer, small business site:** from about $1,500 to $5,000
- **Freelancer or small studio, custom site with more pages or features:** $5,000 to $15,000
- **Agency, larger custom builds, online stores or web apps:** $15,000 and up

My own small business websites start from $1,500, and every project gets a fixed quote before we start. You can also spread the cost over monthly payments.

## What actually changes the price

**Number of pages and content.** Five pages is a different job to fifty. Content is also the biggest hidden cost: if you need help with copy and photos, budget for it.

**Custom design vs a template.** A template is faster and cheaper. Custom design makes you look like *you*, which matters more in competitive markets.

**Features.** Bookings, online payments, member areas, integrations with your CRM or stock system all add time.

**Platform.** WordPress, Squarespace, Shopify, Webflow and custom code all have different trade-offs. I wrote about [choosing between Shopify and Squarespace](/thoughts/shopify-vs-squarespace-australia) if you're selling online.

**SEO and speed.** A site that nobody finds is an expensive brochure. Make sure technical SEO is included, not an upsell.

## The ongoing costs nobody mentions

A website isn't a one-off purchase. Budget for:

- **Domain:** around $20 to $40 a year for a .com.au
- **Hosting or platform fees:** roughly $10 to $60 a month depending on the platform
- **Maintenance:** updates, backups and security, either your time or a care plan
- **Improvements:** small changes as your business evolves

I broke these down in more detail in [website maintenance costs in Australia](/thoughts/website-maintenance-costs-australia).

## How to avoid overpaying (or underpaying)

1. **Ask for a fixed quote.** Hourly estimates have a way of growing.
2. **Check who actually does the work.** With agencies, it's often not the person who sold it to you.
3. **Make sure you own everything.** Domain, hosting account, design files and content should be in your name.
4. **Ask what happens after launch.** Training, support and who to call when something breaks.
5. **Look at real work.** Not mockups, live sites you can click through.

If you're a Perth small business that needs a professional, fast, findable website, a realistic starting budget is $1,500 to $5,000, plus around $30 a month to run it. If that's a stretch right now, start lean and grow it. That's exactly what my [small business page](/start) is for.
