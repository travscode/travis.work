I've built AI experiences for Melbourne Airport and the Australian Open, and AI products at IOOKI Labs. But the most valuable AI work for small businesses is usually far less flashy. Here's where it actually pays off.

## Start with the boring stuff

The best AI projects remove a task someone hates doing every day:

- **Inbox triage:** sorting enquiries, drafting replies for you to approve
- **Quotes and proposals:** first drafts from your notes and price list
- **Data entry:** pulling details from emails, forms and invoices into your systems
- **Summaries:** meeting notes, call transcripts, weekly reports
- **Scheduling and follow-ups:** reminders and booking confirmations

## Customer-facing AI, done carefully

A chatbot trained on your services, prices and FAQs can answer common questions at 2am. The keys: keep it honest about being AI, give it a clear handover to a human, and test it before you trust it.

## How to avoid a wasted AI project

1. **Pick one problem** with a clear payoff, like hours saved per week
2. **Prototype in days,** using your real data
3. **Measure it** against how things work now
4. **Keep humans in the loop** for anything customer-facing or financial
5. **Mind your data:** use providers that don't train on your information

## A realistic first step

Write down every repetitive task in your week and how long each takes. The top one or two are usually your first AI project.
