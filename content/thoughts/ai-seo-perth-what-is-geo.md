Every few months marketing invents a new acronym. This time it's actually worth paying attention to. Here's a plain-English guide for Perth business owners.

## The acronyms, decoded

- **SEO:** search engine optimisation. Ranking in Google's traditional results.
- **GEO:** generative engine optimisation. Getting included in answers written by AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews.
- **AEO:** answer engine optimisation. Basically the same idea as GEO, with a focus on direct answers.
- **"AI SEO":** the catch-all term most people search for.

## Why it matters now

When someone searches Google, they see ten or more options. When they ask an AI, they usually get two or three. That's a much smaller front page, and it's being decided right now while most businesses aren't paying attention.

## What GEO work actually involves

1. **An AI visibility audit.** Asking the major AI tools the questions your customers ask and recording whether you appear.
2. **Clear service and location pages.** One page per thing you do, written plainly.
3. **Answer-ready content.** FAQs and guides that answer real questions directly.
4. **Structured data.** Labelling your business, services, prices and FAQs in code.
5. **Reputation building.** Reviews and mentions on sites AI already trusts.
6. **Monthly tracking.** Re-testing prompts to see your share of answers grow.

## Do you need it yet?

Ask yourself: would a customer ask an AI to recommend someone like me? If you're a local service business (trades, health, hospitality, professional services, creative services) the answer is increasingly yes.

The best part: most GEO work also improves your regular SEO. You're not choosing one or the other.

## Watch out for

Anyone guaranteeing you'll be "recommended by ChatGPT." No one controls what AI models say. What a good provider can do is measurably improve how clearly and how often you're described, and report on it honestly.
