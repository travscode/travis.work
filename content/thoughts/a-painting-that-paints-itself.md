I had such a blast yesterday at [Scitech](https://www.scitech.org.au/), creating a painting live with a room full of super lively kids using nothing but their thoughts and ideas.

no paints, no crayons… and the best part? no messy clean up.

Just kids shouting out whatever popped into their heads and watching a painting paint itself… it’s pretty neat. it figures out what colours, shapes and movement to use based on whatever they shouted at it.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bJwM_GrM-TI)

We chatted about creativity, technology and how to figure out which ideas are actually worth making…

But my personal fave idea of the day?  
When a little girl shouted out: “Ice cream robots”

On my drive home, I kept thinking about how cool it is talking to kids. There’s something incredibly inspiring about a room full of young minds who haven’t yet learned to think in impossibilities.

The image below is just a small cut from the painting as the kids were sharing their ideas and watching it translate and paint their thoughts live.

I love how colourful, light and bright their thoughts turned out to be.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*R9ZH7ufVluxG6PL_17014Q.jpeg)

I reckon “ice cream robot” is probably a good name for how it turned out.
