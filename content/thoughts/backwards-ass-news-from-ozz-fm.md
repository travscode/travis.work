## The Backwards Ass News (on Ozz.fm)

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*6P7A-uWT3pJ4E-adP5w_LA.jpeg)

I realise I’m stating the obvious, but reading the news these days is so damn depressing.  
And yet — like some sick daily ritual — we keep doing it.  
Scrolling, clicking, swallowing headlines like bitter pills.

So I decided to flip the script. Literally.

I built a new segment for [Ozz.fm](https://ozz.fm), my [half-mad hobby radio project](https://medium.com/@travisaweerts/introducing-ozz-fm-music-for-your-third-ear-aaf2a2ea7968), where I programmed my news commentator to read today’s headlines backwards.  
All generated live, straight from the firehose.

Why? Because the news *is* backwards.  
It’s all doom, all gloom... totally upside-down.  
And maybe, just maybe, hearing it in reverse might reveal the madness for what it really is.

So I call it: **Backwards Ass News.**

This morning, on my drive to work, I fired it up. No plan. No filters. Just pure chaos.  
And what came out of the speakers… honestly left me speechless.

When you strip away structure…when the words start falling out of order… they stop being sentences and start being symptoms.  
Each one lands heavier.  
Each one demands to be seen.  
You can *feel* the negativity, how it infects the air. It’s like holding a mirror up to the collective anxiety of a world gone sideways.

This is the language we feed ourselves every day.  
So is it any wonder we’re all convinced the world’s on fire?

Maybe it’s time to turn the volume down on the doom,  
and turn the art back up.
