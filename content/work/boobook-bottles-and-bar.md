## The brief

A historic bottle shop reborn as a European-style wine bar. The brand had to honour more than a century of history while feeling lively and modern.

## The work

I crafted a visual identity and digital experience that celebrates BOOBOOK's curated collection, making it easy for customers to discover, sip and take home something special.
