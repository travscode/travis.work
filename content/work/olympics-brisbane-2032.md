## Context

In December 2022, the Brisbane 2032 organising committee appointed VMLY&R Brisbane to create the Olympic and Paralympic emblems and visual brand strategy, leading a consortium with Landor & Fitch, Kantar Public and BlackCard.

## My part

I worked on web development and UI for the Brisbane 2032 site, built with Next.js. Work at this scale is all about precision: accessibility, performance and consistency with a brand the whole world will see.
