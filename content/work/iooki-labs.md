## What IOOKI does

IOOKI is a research lab and studio. We prototype what doesn't exist yet and turn the promising ideas into real products: interpreter training, smart glasses with voice and vision AI, AI microlearning, booking workflows and more.

## My role

I work across the stack: AI and voice development, brand identity, the website and the product UI/UX.

## Recognition

IOOKI was one of five finalists, chosen from 22 applicants, at the first Australian chapter of the UN/ITU AI for Good Innovation Factory, held in Perth in March 2025.
