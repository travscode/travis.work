## Context

The Raveonettes released two albums on Columbia, Chain Gang of Love and Pretty in Black, which featured guests including Ronnie Spector, Maureen Tucker and Martin Rev.

## My part

I worked for a number of bands back in the day, but this one was special. I'm a big fan of their music and was lucky enough to design and build a site that helped them promote it.
