## What I did

Back in the early days I worked on pretty much everything technical: the booth apps, the websites, the server side, social sharing and the event software that kept it all running on the night.

## Why it stuck with me

Smilebooth is still one of the things I'm most proud of being part of. Total bucket list kind of project. Amazing people, brands and celebrities naturally gravitate towards it. There's something about Smilebooth that's pure magic.

## Today

Smilebooth runs experiences in Atlanta, Austin, Chicago, Houston, LA, Nashville, NYC, San Francisco and Seattle for brands like Google Pixel, Target, Oculus, Sony Alpha, Adidas and House of Vans.
