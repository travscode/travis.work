## The idea

Most photo apps are about making things look perfect. MYSTIC* was about seeing the world a little differently and opening your eyes to the small moments that make life special.

## What I did

Everything: the concept, the brand, the interface and the development, plus the launch marketing.

## What happened

Apple selected MYSTIC* to feature globally. It charted in the top 10 across dozens of countries and climbed as high as #2 in Germany.
