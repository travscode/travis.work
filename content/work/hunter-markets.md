## The brief

Hunter Markets had already built a loyal community around second-hand fashion and keeping clothes out of landfill. The goal was to give that community a place to buy and sell between market days, without losing the social, treasure-hunt feel of the real thing.

## What we built

A social marketplace app for iOS and Android: listings that feel like browsing a rack, simple selling, and a design that puts the clothes first. I led the design and development alongside the Hunter Markets and Dilate teams.

## Launch week

We launched with Sopha Dopha selling her wardrobe for charity, and somehow our little indie app shot to #5 in Shopping on the Australian App Store almost overnight. Pretty surreal.
