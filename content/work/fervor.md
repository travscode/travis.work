## The story

Mike and Callum's wine journey spans decades of relationships across Western Australia and Victoria. Their first wines were made in Geelong before they settled in Denmark, WA. The brand needed to feel as genuine as they are.

## The name

"Fervor" captures the passion behind everything they make. It's short, spirited and memorable on a shelf.

## The identity

The visual identity and packaging reflect the elegance of the Great Southern, balanced with a sense of exploration and modern design. Classic and vibrant, just like the wines. Callum was a 2024 Young Gun of Wine Top Winemakers finalist.
