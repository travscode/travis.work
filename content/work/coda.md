## The challenge

Coda already had fans. The job was to make the labels feel more confident and considered on the shelf, while staying recognisably Coda.

## The work

I developed a visual language that's bold but refined, balancing modern energy with a sense of timeless quality. The result speaks to people discovering the range for the first time and to those who've been drinking it for years.

## About Skigh

Skigh Wine sources fruit from Margaret River, Great Southern and Geographe, with a cellar door at Yallingup Siding. Winemaker Skigh McManus was a 2025 Young Gun of Wine Top Winemakers finalist.
