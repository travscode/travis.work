## The festival

What started in the 1950s as a fundraiser linking local artists with the volunteer bushfire brigade has grown into one of Perth's longest-running community festivals: an exhibition in Darlington Hall and a fair on the oval next door.

## My part

I design the festival's print materials and act as a consultant for artists, helping them design their own festival materials so the whole event looks its best.
