## The idea

Foundation Day 2024 had a music theme: goodness goes on tour. The site needed to feel like a headline act, something people would want to open, share and come back to as the day rolled across time zones.

## What I built

I designed and built the experience with WebGL visuals, Spotify integration, voice modelling and chat. It became a hub that connected teams, shared stories and turned 24 hours of service into a worldwide movement.

## Context

VML describes Worldwide Foundation Day as the industry's largest synchronised charitable corporate impact day, with offices around the world closing to normal business for the day.
