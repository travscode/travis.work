## The problem

Certification exams for interpreters are high-stakes and practice partners are hard to find. Candidates need realistic, repeatable practice with honest feedback.

## What I did

I led the journey across product strategy, design and development, crafting a platform that blends AI with real-world interpreter training. I also helped refine the pitch.

## Recognition

That pitch helped earn a place among the five finalists (under IOOKI) at the AI for Good Innovation Factory Australia, backed by the UN's ITU, in Perth in 2025.
