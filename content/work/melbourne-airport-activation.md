## The idea

Airport kiosks are useful and almost nobody enjoys them. For Christmas, the airport wanted something warmer: a character you could just talk to, who could point you to your gate, a coffee or the toilets.

## What I built

I developed the full application. It combines real-time voice processing, a carefully designed personality, phoneme-based lip-syncing so the koala's mouth matched its words, and privacy-safe facial detection so it knew when someone was actually engaging.

## What I learned

Putting conversational AI in a public space raises real questions about transparency, data and whether people know they're talking to a machine. I wrote about it in [Are we talking to the walls now?](https://medium.com/@travisaweerts/are-we-talking-to-the-walls-now-0fe158b442ea)
