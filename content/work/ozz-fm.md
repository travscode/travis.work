## The idea

Radio used to be the place you discovered music with other people. OZZ FM brings that back with a twist: anyone can spin up a station, give it a voice and personality, and shape the sound together with their listeners.

## What I did

I worked on creative direction, brand identity, UI/UX and the app and AI development. The tagline says it best: music for your third ear.
