## The moment

Activations at the Australian Open live or die in a few seconds. Fans are walking past with a drink in one hand and a schedule in the other. The experience had to be instantly understandable, fun to watch and quick to deliver something worth sharing.

## What I did

I worked on the UI/UX development for the photobooth: the flow from walk-up to finished portrait, the on-screen guidance and the moment of reveal. Generative AI did the styling; the interface made it feel effortless.

## Why it worked

Good activations hide the technology and amplify the fun. People didn't need to know anything about AI. They just stepped up, smiled and walked away with a tennis-themed portrait of themselves. #dreamjob
