## The foundation

The Make Yourself Foundation is a 501(c)(3) charity funded by the band's touring and royalty income and by fan auctions of tickets and memorabilia, supporting causes from Surfrider Foundation to Heal the Bay.

## My part

I designed and built the foundation's website in 2005. To this day I still can't believe I got the chance to work with them.
