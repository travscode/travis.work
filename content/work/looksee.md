## The problem

Choosing floors, blinds or lights from a tiny swatch is a gamble. People hesitate, retailers lose sales and everyone wishes they could just see it.

## What we built

LookSee lets you upload a photo of your space and see the product in it, properly lit and to scale. I led creative direction, brand identity, UI/UX and app development.
