## The problem

Choosing floors, blinds or lights from a tiny swatch is a gamble. People hesitate, retailers lose sales and everyone wishes they could just see it.

## What we built

LookSee is an AI tool that lets you upload a photo of your space and see the product in it, properly lit and to scale. As one of the founders, my focus has been creative direction, brand identity and UI/UX, working alongside the rest of the team on the product.

## How it works

1. Take a photo of your room, or upload one you already have.
2. Pick a product, or paste a link to one from a retailer's site.
3. LookSee places it in your room with realistic lighting and scale, so you can compare options before you buy.

It already has. The LookSee beta is now open to everyone at [trylooksee.com](https://trylooksee.com), so you can sign up and start trying products in your own space today.
