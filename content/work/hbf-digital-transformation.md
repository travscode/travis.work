## The program

HBF completed a multi-year transformation in 2024, replacing legacy systems, including a core insurance mainframe, with modern cloud platforms: a new core insurance system, CRM, marketing automation, an employee service tool, an enhanced myHBF portal and a redesigned website.

## My part

I worked on UI/UX design and web development across member-facing interfaces, helping make everyday tasks like managing cover and claims feel simple, and supporting a joined-up view for the staff who help members.

## Why it matters

When around 80% of interactions happen digitally, the website and portal *are* the relationship. Clarity and trust aren't nice-to-haves at that scale.
