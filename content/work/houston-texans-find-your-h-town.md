## Home town

When a buddy asked if I wanted to jump in and help turn my home town of Houston into one giant scavenger hunt, celebrating the city's history and those iconic little blue tiles scattered all over town, I was an immediate... "hell yeah man".

Since the 1920s, small blue and white tiles set into Houston's curbs and sidewalks have spelled out the names of its streets. They're some of the city's oldest landmarks, and they're quietly disappearing. The Blue Tile Project started with a pretty simple idea: preserve them, and use them to tell Houston's story.

![The H-Town blue tile sign in front of the downtown Houston skyline](/assets/media/houston_texans_htown_blue_tile_sign_skyline.jpg)

## Find Your H-Town

Fast forward to 2026, and that little spark turned into **Find Your H-Town**, a citywide scavenger hunt with the Houston Texans to celebrate the reveal of their new HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniforms, where the stripes, numbers and nameplates are all built from Houston's blue street tiles.

From November 1 to 19, the Texans have been hiding 25 blue tile signs (or "drops") all over the city, with clues pushed through the Texans app. The first person to find a sign keeps it, and every find is entered to win even more. Tickets, sideline passes, tailgate gear, a signed Andre Johnson football, and even Bud Light beer for a year. Every sign that's found lands on the Blue Tile Map, credited to the finder and the Texans.

## The Blue Tile Map

The hunt is powered by Blue Tile Project, and so is the map that goes with it: a public archive of Houston's collective memory. Houstonians can drop their own tile on the exact spot their story happened (the block they grew up on, their tailgate spot, a first date), tell it by typing or just saying it out loud, and add a photo and the song that goes with the memory. Spot a real blue tile in the sidewalk? Snap it and it goes on the map, credited to you.

![Find Your H-Town presented by Bud Light](/assets/media/find_your_htown_presented_by_bud_light.jpg)

## Still on

It all builds to the Rivalries game against the Colts on November 19, when the new uniforms hit the field for the first time. So if you're out and about in Houston, there's still time to find one of the last drops.
