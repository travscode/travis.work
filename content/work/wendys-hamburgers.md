## The launch

Wendy's opened its first Australian restaurant at the Paradise Centre in Surfers Paradise on 15 January 2025, with master franchisee Flynn Group planning 200 restaurants nationwide.

## My part

I built the Australian website to introduce the brand and support the launch. A global brand entering a new market gets one first impression, so speed, clarity and appetite appeal were everything.
