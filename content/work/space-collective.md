## The brief

Space Collective needed a presence that reflected their collaborative spirit, creative intelligence and commitment to raising the standard of housing and public design.

## The work

The identity feels confident yet refined, just like their buildings. The website lets the work lead, with generous imagery and a calm structure that makes it easy to explore projects from Alma Square to Monelli Cafe.
