# Review requests and directory listings

Copy for getting Google reviews and filling out Google Business Profile and directory listings.

**Before you send anything:** in Google Business Profile, click **Ask for reviews** (or "Get more reviews") and copy the short link it gives you. Paste it wherever you see `[REVIEW LINK]`.

---

## 1. Review request email

Send each one personally from your own inbox, not as a mail-out. Change the first line for each client so it mentions their actual project.

**Subject:** Quick favour?

> Hi [First name],
>
> Hope things are going well with [project, e.g. the new site / the festival / the app]. I really enjoyed working on it with you.
>
> I'm trying to get my business showing up better on Google, and reviews from real clients make a big difference. If you were happy with the work, would you mind leaving a quick review? It takes about a minute:
>
> [REVIEW LINK]
>
> A sentence or two is plenty. If you can mention what we worked on (e.g. "website design" or "app development") and that you're in Perth, that helps even more... but anything is appreciated.
>
> And if there's anything you weren't happy with, I'd honestly rather hear it from you directly.
>
> Thanks heaps,
> Travis
> 0422 188 213 · travis.work

### Follow-up (send once, about a week later, only if they haven't reviewed)

**Subject:** Re: Quick favour?

> Hi [First name], just bumping this in case it got buried. Here's the link again if you have a minute: [REVIEW LINK]
>
> No worries at all if not. Thanks again!
> Travis

### Text message version (for clients you usually text)

> Hey [First name], Travis here. Would you mind leaving me a quick Google review for the [project] work? It really helps my business get found. Takes a minute: [REVIEW LINK] Thanks heaps!

### Tips

- **Best timing:** right after a launch or a happy client email. For past clients, send in small batches of 3 to 5 a week, not all at once. A steady trickle looks more natural to Google.
- **Reply to every review** within a day or two, naming the project and the service (e.g. "Loved designing the new website for..."). Your replies help with local rankings too.
- **Rules to stay inside:** never offer discounts or gifts for reviews, don't write reviews for clients, and don't ask only the clients you know were happy. Breaking these is against Google's policies and can get reviews removed.
- **Who to ask first:** recent web and design clients in Perth. Their reviews help most with "web designer perth" style searches.
